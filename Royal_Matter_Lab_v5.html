Royal Field Guide

Element: One kind of atom. Cannot be chemically broken into simpler substances.

Compound: Different elements chemically bonded in a fixed ratio.

Homogeneous mixture: Uniform throughout; components are not chemically bonded to each other.

Heterogeneous mixture: Not uniform throughout; distinct parts or phases may be observed.

A single observation may be inconclusive. A chemical change can help identify a material, but bubbling alone does not prove a reaction.
